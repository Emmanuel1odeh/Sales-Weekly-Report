# Sales Weekly Report

### Overview

This project analyzes the weekly dataset of the marketing team of Plinth Construction and Real estate to uncover patterns and insights that can inform the company in better decision making and strategic execution.

### Objective
* Identify the relationship between the current marketing strategy and sales
* Increase the rate of sales of the company's lands and properties

### Key Findings
* Marketing via ads produce better results compared to flyer distribution
* Digital marketing proves to be more reliable than physical marketing
